Privacy policy

Family Schedule Bot, last updated 8 October 2026

Who runs this bot

Family Schedule Bot is a private tool run by Eugene Neo for his own family. It is not available to the public.

What data it uses

With permission given through Google sign-in, the bot reads events from the family's Google Calendar, such as event titles, dates and times. It uses this only to send schedule reminders to family members on WhatsApp.

How data is stored

The bot keeps only what it needs to work, such as the Google sign-in token. Calendar data is not sold, shared with anyone outside the family, or used for advertising.

Removing access

You can remove the bot's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
